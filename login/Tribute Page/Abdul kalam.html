Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Missile Man of India

About Him

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was one of India's most respected scientists and the 11th President of India. He played a major role in India's missile and space programs and inspired millions of students through his speeches and books.

Known as the "Missile Man of India", he dedicated his life to science, education, and national development. His humility, hard work, and vision continue to motivate young people.

I admire Dr. Kalam because of his dedication, simplicity, leadership, and commitment to helping students achieve their dreams.

"Dream, Dream, Dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."
